# START HERE — your first ten minutes (sample)

This is the free sample of the WriterVault setup path. It takes you from an empty folder to a working manuscript pipeline in about ten minutes. You need Obsidian (or any Markdown editor), this repository copied somewhere you can write, and the working title of whatever you are writing. No plugins, no accounts, no network steps.

> This repository is a **free sample** of the full WriterVault kit. Files referenced below that are not in this repository (`templates/manuscript-project.md`, `dashboards/`, `reference/`, the other templates) ship with the full kit on [Gumroad](https://nandminc.gumroad.com/l/writervault-kit) (current $1 intro price — price may change).

## Minute 0–2: read what this vault is not

- [ ] Open `README.md` and read the "Honest scope — what this is not" section.

Two honest minutes now prevent ten confused hours later: this vault is structure. It does not write, edit, market or pitch anything, and it promises no outcome — you bring the words.

## Minute 2–4: copy the sample template into your vault

Copy `templates/chapter-card.md` into the vault where you will actually write. It is the canonical contract for the chapter log: the columns, their meanings, the word-count rollups and the front-matter keys. Keep the original untouched and work in copies, so a clean template is always one copy away.

In the full kit this step copies nine templates — project card, scene card, character dossier, setting entry, continuity guard, series canon log, submission tracker and query-letter skeleton join the chapter card.

## Minute 4–6: create your project

- [ ] Make a project note for your book: Title, Genre, Target words, and one line adopting the status ladder: "Status vocabulary: idea → draft → revise → polished → submitted — closed, lowercase."

`Target words` is the denominator of every progress percentage you will ever compute in this vault. Set it now; change it only if the plan of the book genuinely changes.

## Minute 6–8: add your first three chapters

- [ ] Copy `templates/chapter-card.md` into a chapter log note for your book.
- [ ] Add three rows: chapter numbers 1, 2 and 3, working titles, POV characters, `idea` status, zero words, empty Last edited.

Three `idea` rows are a manuscript. Everything else in this vault is counting.

## Minute 8–10: look at a finished one

- [ ] Open `worked-example/manuscript-sample.md` and read the filled project card, the 24-row chapter log and the sprint log. It is **fictional sample data** — Wren Calloway and *The Salt Cartographer* are made up — but the shapes are exactly what your first month should look like.
- [ ] Copy the shapes, replace the content, keep the columns.

That is the whole system: a project card, a chapter log, a bible that pins facts down, a tracker for what goes out, and two minutes a week to keep the numbers true. The full kit exists to make each of those five things unambiguous.
