## What a manuscript vault is

Most writing tools want you to draft inside them. A manuscript vault inverts that: your manuscript lives as files you own, and the vault is the structure around it — one row per chapter in a chapter log, scene cards when you want beat-level tracking, a story bible that pins facts down, and a tracker for what goes out to agents. Structure only; you bring the words.

The organizing idea is a **five-word status ladder** — idea, draft, revise, polished, submitted — a closed vocabulary used everywhere in the vault, so a status rollup is a count, not an interpretation. That closed vocabulary, plus plain pipe tables, is what makes a zero-plugin Markdown vault genuinely queryable.

## Install

Copy the contents of this repository into an Obsidian vault, or into any folder of Markdown files. If you use Obsidian, open the folder as a vault and the files appear in the sidebar; if you use another Markdown editor, open the files directly. That is the whole installation: one copy, zero plugins, zero accounts.

## How the vault works

Your manuscript lives as one row per chapter in a chapter log, kept to the canonical column spec in `templates/chapter-card.md`. The word-count rollups are four plain formulas: sum the chapter words, divide by your target, subtract to get what remains, divide the target by the chapter count for a per-chapter yardstick. Around the manuscript sits the story bible, where continuity guards lock the facts that must never drift — when a scene contradicts a guard, fix the scene, never the guard.

When a manuscript is ready to go out, the submission tracker's response-day math turns agent silence into numbers — average, median, fastest, slowest, request rate, days open. Pace math included: 1,667 words/day is NaNoWriMo's own published daily goal for a 50k month.

## Honest scope — what this is not

This vault is not a writing course and not writing advice; it does not tell you what to write or how to fix a scene. It is not an agent-finder or a submission service; it does not look up agencies or send anything on your behalf. It is not AI — the vault does not write, edit, market, or pitch your book; your words are your words, entered by you, stored on your device.
